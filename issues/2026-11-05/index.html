The Morning Picture

Grok Bot answers the phone and shops; Starlink Mobile picks Qualcomm for a hybrid U.S. network

Elon Musk spent Wednesday and overnight pitching Grok Bot as a personal agent that can stay on a live phone call, answer mid-call questions from chat, research purchases, place orders and handle returns. He also amplified SpaceX's Starlink Mobile plan for a fully integrated U.S. satellite-and-terrestrial phone network built on Qualcomm Dragonwing cellular silicon starting in 2027, and pointed early Starlink customers to free Standard kit upgrades.

Overnight he kept the product drumbeat going – dentist appointments by phone, a pocket "GrokPuck" demo, and a one-line reaction to a congressional call for pausing advanced AI. The through-line across the window was agents, spectrum and dishes.

I · Grok Bot & AI

"It's like hiring an amazing assistant"

Musk quote-posted mid-call phone demos, a shopping flow that researches and returns items, users consolidating onto Grok Bot, and a Huxley-flavored reply to a call for pausing advanced AI.

6+Musk posts overnight and Wednesday promoting Grok Bot calls, shopping and assistants
14%Insurance renewal hike a user asked Grok Bot to challenge mid-call
Chat cardHow the bot takes a mid-call question without hanging up

At 12:44 p.m. EST Wednesday, Musk wrote "Try Grok @Bot. It's like hiring an amazing assistant!" over a demo from Texas Twins Dad, who said Grok Bot called his car insurer about a 14% renewal hike with zero accidents. When the representative asked a question mid-call, a card popped up in chat; he typed an answer and the bot kept talking. "It's like passing a note to my assistant while they're still on the phone," the user wrote.

Dentist on the line

At 2:59 a.m. EST Thursday Musk posted "Grok @Bot can call your dentist on the phone!" quoting Arthur MacWaters, who said the bot was calling a dentist to check whether the office took his insurance. Five minutes earlier he wrote "Everyone is using Grok @Bot," amplifying Min-Liang Tan, who showed a pocket "GrokPuck" hardware demo that switches bots with a swipe.

Quick Hit

Leaving Muse. Engineer Shubham Saboo said he moved everything from OpenClaw, Hermes and Muse onto Grok Bot, calling it a clear winner for personal agents on interface, features and shipping speed. Musk replied "Try Grok @Bot!" at 12:30 a.m. EST Thursday.

Shop, track, return

"Grok @Bot will research purchase ideas, bring back a selection, order them, track the purchases and return anything you don't want," Musk wrote at 10:00 a.m. EST Wednesday, quoting Andrew Milich on an improved buying flow. The underlying @bot product post invites users to find deals, compare items and have Bot track orders.

"It's a Brave New World"

At 2:46 a.m. EST Thursday, Musk quoted Rep. Yassamin Ansari with "It's a Brave New World" after she called for a pause on advanced AI development, regulation of the industry, and a ban on artificial superintelligence.

II · Starlink Mobile

Qualcomm Dragonwing for a nationwide hybrid network

Starlink framed a fully integrated U.S. satellite-and-terrestrial phone network; Qualcomm will supply Dragonwing 5G infrastructure silicon from 2027, and early dish customers get free Standard kits.

2027Year Starlink said terrestrial 5G base stations using Qualcomm chips begin deploying
64T64RMassive multiple-input multiple-output radio configuration in Qualcomm's Dragonwing platforms
FreeStandard kit upgrades Musk flagged for early first-generation Starlink customers

Starlink posted at 8:32 a.m. EST Wednesday that "Starlink Mobile is building the first fully-integrated US nationwide satellite and terrestrial network that will keep people connected on their phones no matter where they are," with 5G base stations leveraging Qualcomm chips starting in 2027. Qualcomm chief executive Cristiano Amon wrote that SpaceX selected Qualcomm Dragonwing to accelerate Starlink Mobile's nationwide terrestrial 5G network and a path to "AI-native 6G." Qualcomm's press release says the multi-year deal uses Dragonwing QRU100 and QDU100 platforms with 64 transmit and 64 receive (64T64R) Massive MIMO radios, with U.S. nationwide deployment beginning in 2027 and tens of thousands of sites planned.

Musk quoted Starlink at 10:04 a.m. EST:

"At last there will be one network with good signal everywhere!"
Elon Musk · 10:04 a.m. EST Wednesday

Four minutes earlier he separately wrote "Qualcomm's technology is excellent."

Free dishes for early customers

Musk wrote "Free new dish for all early Starlink customers" at 10:02 a.m. EST Wednesday, quoting Sawyer Merritt's report that eligible customers still on a first-generation kit can receive a new Standard kit at no cost, with a free mount and optional free installation, invited by email. Starlink's support article carries the same eligibility language.

On the wire

AI pause ask. Rep. Yassamin Ansari called for a pause on advanced AI development and a ban on artificial superintelligence (post).

Takeaways

  1. Musk spent the window selling Grok Bot as a phone-and-shopping assistant that stays on live calls and can research, order and return purchases.
  2. Users he amplified said the bot handled an insurance renewal mid-call and a dentist insurance check without forcing a human takeover.
  3. Starlink Mobile is staking a hybrid U.S. phone network on Qualcomm Dragonwing silicon, with terrestrial 5G sites aimed at 2027.
  4. Early first-generation Starlink customers were offered free Standard kit upgrades with a free mount and optional free install.
  5. Overnight Musk answered a call to pause advanced AI and ban artificial superintelligence with "It's a Brave New World."
