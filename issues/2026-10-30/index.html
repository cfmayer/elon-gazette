The Morning Picture

Telepathy first, then Blindsight

"Neuralink's first product is called Telepathy. It enables high-speed communication with computers or humans simply by thinking. Output bandwidth will vastly exceed the fastest human speech or typing in a few years," Elon Musk posted at 12:06 a.m. EDT Friday. He was quoting Neuralink's post that its engineers had visited a speech restoration study participant for a 72-hour hackathon "to enhance the experience of using Neuralink to communicate with thought."

Four minutes later he added: "Our second product is called Blindsight and it will, as the name suggests, enable even those who are completely blind or have never seen to see. It is ready from an engineering standpoint. Just waiting on regulatory approval." Blindsight received the FDA's Breakthrough Device Designation two years ago, which speeds review but is not an approval, TeslaNorth noted. At 12:36 a.m. Musk wrote: "Hopefully, in a few years, we will be able to shunt signals from the motor and sensory areas of the brain to the limbs and enable people to use their hands and walk again."

I · SpaceX & Starlink

The FCC moves on satellite-to-phone spectrum

Commissioners proposed 482 MHz more for the service behind Starlink Mobile, Musk looked to Von Neumann probes, and Starlink began local pricing.

482 MHzmore spectrum proposed for satellite-to-phone service
5 → 13bands that would be eligible under the FCC plan
±20%range of Starlink's new local Residential pricing

The Federal Communications Commission voted on Thursday to propose making an additional 482 megahertz of spectrum available for Supplemental Coverage from Space, the satellite-to-phone service behind Starlink Mobile, and to expand the eligible bands from five to 13, according to the FCC. Chairman Brendan Carr and Commissioners Anna Gomez and Olivia Trusty approved the notice of proposed rulemaking, which would also drop the rule's leasing and geographically independent area requirements. "With today's proposal, we take our most comprehensive step yet to modernize the FCC's rules for direct-to-device," Carr said (FCC). The plan now goes to public comment, and final rules would need another vote.

AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile had wanted 290 MHz cut from the plan, Teslarati posted. In a filing posted by Sawyer Merritt at 2:51 p.m. EDT Thursday, SpaceX said "the misplaced desire to harm a competitor has led many incumbents to oppose modernized rules that would bring direct-to-cell service to underserved border communities."

"Von Neumann probes by 2100"

"Our civilization would be winning wondrously if we are exploring the galaxy with Von Neumann probes by 2100! Larry Page might be right. He has correctly predicted many things," Musk wrote at 11:19 a.m. EDT Thursday. He was quoting a clip in which Microsoft's Mustafa Suleyman recalls Page telling him at SpaceX that by the end of the century he would be exploring the galaxy at the head of a von Neumann probe. Musk replied "Next-level" at 1:12 a.m. Friday to a post calling Page "e/acc."

Quick Hit

Prices by neighborhood. Starlink told Residential customers that prices will now move "based on supply of capacity and demand," with reviews "roughly quarterly"; in Rolla, Missouri, the Residential Max plan now defaults to $156 a month, up from $130, PCMag reported.

Starship Flight 15 slips

"Starship Flight 15 is now NET November 12 per a new CADENA high profile missions slide," Space Notices posted Thursday, citing a range-planning chart. SpaceX has not announced a date, and Musk had said a day earlier that Ship 42 "will be the first tower catch."

II · Tesla & Autonomy

"The Terafab project is going well"

Korean suppliers described record speed on the chip project, Tesla's board urged votes for Musk and two directors, and Italy backed FSD approval.

1Mwafers a month targeted within 10 years, according to The Elec
1.74Mpaid FSD customers, according to Tesla's board
$403.30TSLA close on Thursday, down 0.2%

"The Terafab project is going well," Musk posted at 1:12 a.m. EDT Friday, quoting a summary of an opinion piece in Korea's The Elec. The outlet wrote that Tesla and SpaceX procurement staff visited about 10 Korean supplier booths at SEMICON West, that more than half signed non-disclosure agreements, and that the target is 1 million wafers a month within 10 years. "Even when we send inquiries in the early morning hours in the U.S., we receive responses within 30 minutes to an hour," a supplier official told the paper. "I've never seen this level of execution speed in the semiconductor industry," said another.

The board asks for votes for Musk, Denholm and Straubel

In a letter to shareholders filed with the SEC on Thursday, Tesla's board asked investors to vote for the re-election of Musk, Robyn Denholm and JB Straubel at the Dec. 3 annual meeting (SEC). The letter cites 1.74 million paid FSD customers, an attach rate of about 75% on new North American deliveries, more than $100 billion in trailing 12-month revenue, more than 10 million vehicles delivered and a total shareholder return of 35,566% since the IPO.

Quick Hit

"Very high too." To a list of expected four-year profit growth that put SpaceX first at +7,923%, Musk replied at 11:15 p.m. EDT Thursday: "Tesla will be very high too."

"Much appreciated"

"Very positive and constructive conversation with @elonmusk today. Italy is looking with great interest at the opportunities offered by assisted driving technologies, with a focus on innovation and road safety, and supports a swift EU approval of Tesla Assisted Driving," Italy's deputy prime minister, Matteo Salvini, posted. "Much appreciated. Thank you!" Musk replied at 9:02 a.m. EDT Thursday.

Tesla slips 0.2%

Tesla shares closed Thursday at $403.30, down 0.2%, according to Yahoo Finance data. SpaceX's SPCX fell 0.92% to $143.89 (Yahoo Finance).

III · X, Grok & AI

"Grok 4.8 will not be a disappointment"

Musk said the next model is late but useful, pitched Grok Bot as an employee, and weighed in on the Binance theft and on Neuralink and epilepsy.

16days in a row of Grok Bot daily-user growth, per a post Musk quoted
~$6.4Bstolen from Binance hot wallets, according to Elliptic
11blockchains hit in the Binance theft

"I know it's taken longer than expected, but Grok 4.8 will not be a disappointment. We're not optimizing for benchmarks and I really don't care how Grok does on those, but it will be incredibly useful," Musk wrote at 1:17 a.m. EDT Friday, replying to a post that said SpaceXSI "barely has any SOTA models" apart from Voice. To Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis, who posted "No.1," he replied at 7:00 p.m. Thursday: "Well done! When can it be accessed?"

"Hire Grok @Bot to be an employee"

"Worth noting that Grok @Bot has no free tier," Musk posted at 5:39 p.m. EDT Thursday, quoting a post that its daily active users had risen for 16 consecutive days. At 12:33 a.m. Friday he wrote "Hire Grok @Bot to be an employee," and at 12:39 a.m. he answered a user who had it build a concert playlist for his children in 30 seconds: "It's great @Bot."

Quick Hit

"Everyday is Zeroday." Musk posted that at 6:33 p.m. EDT Thursday, quoting a report of outflows from Binance. Hackers took about $6.4 billion from the exchange's hot wallets across 11 blockchains, starting around 4:05 p.m., Elliptic said; Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals and said its SAFU fund will cover losses. At 11:12 p.m. Musk wrote: "Super intelligence agents will at some point do this to gain currency for themselves, not a human."

"Neuralink will solve that with finesse"

To a parent who asked him to "Please fix epilepsy," Musk replied at 12:25 a.m. EDT Friday: "Neuralink will solve that with finesse at some point. Have you considered any of the existing devices? They are a little brutish, but my understanding that they can be effective for some types of epilepsy." At 12:38 a.m. he wrote "Longevity is coming."

Claude, Levie and links

Anthropic said it has trained Claude to flag potential misuse right after it responds, a technique it calls self-assessment; Musk replied "Good idea" at 1:37 a.m. He answered "True" at 1:09 a.m. (post) to Box chief executive Aaron Levie's post that the distance between people working at the AI frontier and everyone else is "only growing farther apart." On X, he wrote at 7:02 p.m. Thursday: "Feedback from my team. Links are just used for improving the algorithm recommendations and making advertising relevance suck less."

On the wire

"Read that sentence again." Musk ended his 12:06 a.m. EDT Telepathy post with those words.
